# Vulcan Mushroom - Great Ideas & Context Ledger

This ledger captures high-value user architectural choices, design directives, and system enhancements for future development context.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Visual Language
- **Calibrated Vulcan Brand Palette:**
  - `#00A06C` — Primary Emerald Voice (interactive anchor, primary CTAs, active indicators)
  - `#8DCCAD` — Soft Sage / Mint (secondary metrics, comparative series, subtle borders)
  - `#D8D2C8` — Warm Stone / Linen (restrained structural borders, grid lines, surface dividers)
  - `#F4EBC3` — Pale Parchment (warm surface container highlights, gentle warnings)
  - `#FFFFFF` — Pure White (clean card substrates, high-contrast modal backdrops)
- **Official 3D Faceted Brand Logo:**
  - Integrated consistently across desktop sidebar, mobile header, login view, and app loading state.
  - Multi-resolution icon set (`icon.png`, `icon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-icon.png`).
- **High-Contrast Eyebrows:**
  - Bold, dark outer container (`#121A14`) with Vulcan emerald border (`#283E2E`), subtle outer elevation ring, and crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`) to eliminate low-contrast pastel washouts.

---

## 2. Deterministic Intelligence & Data Lineage
- **No Synthetic Smoothing:**
  - Zero fabricated, smoothed, or guessed numbers. When data is absent or unverified, display explicit `N/A` or dedicated empty states.
- **Direct Digital Twin Telemetry Binding:**
  - Cultivation, harvest, downtime, and quality analytics reflect direct room sensor feeds and verified farm observation sheets.
- **Interactive Performance Methodology:**
  - Every operational metric on the Performance page includes an interactive `(i)` button opening full calculation formulas, telemetry inputs, domain rules, and industry benchmarks.

---

## 3. Ergonomics & Interaction Standards
- **Tabular Numbers Everywhere (`tnum`):**
  - All numerical counts, kilograms, percentages, and timestamps enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate layout jitter.
- **Quiet by Default:**
  - Detailed breakdown accordions and secondary inspector panels remain collapsed on initial view to preserve operator focus.
- **Dual-Layer Contrast Contract:**
  - Textual metrics use darkened semantic tokens (`#006A46`, `#93000A`, `#645F40`) to maintain WCAG AA (>= 4.5:1) compliance on light surfaces.
