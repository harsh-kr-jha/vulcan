## Explicitly not sourced

No public source was treated as evidence for actual factory capacity, plant layout, asset count or
identity, machine specification, customers, suppliers, project portfolio, order value, schedule,
production rate, quality rate, downtime, inventory, energy use, staffing or financial results.
All such values in this pack are visibly synthetic and anonymized.

Technical process steps and defect modes are plausible test assumptions derived from generic
industrial fabrication practice. They are not claims about InfinexxGen's internal procedures.
