   envelopes, flaps, technical products, engineering products, custom compound mixing,
   automotive/NVH components, aerospace and defence.
2. [MYPOL locations](https://mypol.in/locations) — Mysuru head office at 20P, KRS Road,
   Metagalli Post, Mysuru 570016.
3. [MYPOL certifications](https://mypol.in/certifications) — ISO 9001:2015, IATF 16949,
   ISO 14001:2015 and AS 9100D are publicly listed.
4. [MYPOL company brochure](https://expo.technobiz.org/uploadFiles/exhibitors/00006/418/brochure/brochure00153-153-20231221175820.pdf)
   — more than 100 automotive tube sizes from two-wheeler to tractor/OTR applications and
   rear-wheel dampers for motorcycles.
5. [BIS overview of IS 13098:2024](https://www.services.bis.gov.in/tmp/tbl5_2024-11-19-09-33.pdf)
   — automotive pneumatic tyre tubes, including butyl rubber tubes and air-retention expectations.

## General process basis

6. [US EPA AP-42, Manufacture of Rubber Products](https://www3.epa.gov/ttnchie1/ap42/ch04/draft/d04s12.pdf)
   — generic steps include mixing, milling, extrusion, calendering, curing and finishing; typical
   inputs include raw rubber, carbon black/fillers, process oils, zinc oxide, accelerators,
   antioxidants and sulphur compounds.
